🔎 Internal Review settings

Optimus Internal Review, part of the AutoSetup prototype. The site check below is the same permission as the Checklist Autofill's platform detection: allowing it in one place allows it in both.

Advertiser site check (eCommerce platform)

Each review opens the advertiser's website (and landing page) from the record in a background tab and works out its eCommerce platform from signs in the page (Shopify, WooCommerce, Magento, BigCommerce, Salesforce Commerce Cloud, PrestaShop, Shopware, Wix, Squarespace, OpenCart, Lightspeed, Shoplazza, VTEX, Nuvemshop). The platform is compared with the record and filled in when blank; platform domains (Shopify's *.myshopify.com) are added to the accepted domains. It only reads: it clicks nothing and closes the tab. Chrome needs your permission once, because the advertisers' sites can be any domain.
